# A stay Part II
This was the follow up challenge uploaded (3rd of November, 2026) by TraceLabs after the original A stay challenge. This challenge is also about the previous investigation of Room 79 at the Château Marmont in Los Angeles.

## Description of the challenge
The individual that was in room 79 somehow disappeared. Before the individual  left the room they spend hours photographing the giant billboard located on the hotel's corner of the Sunset Strip.

Even though the Château Marmont has been monitored for three days, the officers never saw the individual leaving through the lobby, garage or bungalows. This made the officers raise suspicion about a potential secret passage that leads away from the Château.

## Investigation Objectives
**1.** Find the Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument number of the building.

**2.** Identify the street number of the building to which the hidden passage leads.

**3.** Identify the hotel originally advertised on the billboard in the 1950s.

**4.** Determine the single word displayed prominently on the advertisement that replaced the Marlboro billboard in the 1990s.

## 1. Historic-Cultural Monument Number
The first objective was to identify the Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument number assigned to the Château Marmont.

The first thing I did was simply a google search for the historical-cultural moment number of the Château Marmont. There were a ton of sources that confirmed that the number was 151.

**-** https://www.flickr.com/photos/7294653@N07/2563770282

**-** https://thatfilmspot.com/locations/chateau-marmont/

### Finding
The finding for the first question is: **151**.

## 2. Identifying the Hidden Passage's Destination
The next objective was to identify the building connected to the suspected hidden passage.

Online I searched for any secret passages that are known about that lead to the Château Marmont. The first thing that popped up was an Instagram post from a random account mentioning it had a passage to a former nightclub located across the street from the hotel: **the Players Club**, which was later known as the Roxbury Club and Pink Taco, which celebraties would use secretly.
Although, Instagram isn't the most reliable source so I went to dig deeper and found another news platform further confirming it:

**-** https://www.instagram.com/p/DNOdgioSXCY/

**-** https://waterandpower.org/Museum2/Chateau_Marmont.html

After finding the building, I went ahead and searched for the address of the current name of the place. This told me both the places mentioned are on the same address: **8225 Sunset Boulevard, Los Angeles, California**.

### Finding
This gave us the street number we were attempting to find for the second question: **8225**.

## 3. Identifying the Original Billboard Advertisement
The third objective was to determine which hotel the billboard originally advertised when it was first installed in the 1950s.

I searched for historical photographs of the Sunset Strip billboard. This took considerably more research, as I initially came across a black-and-white photograph showing an advertisement for the Aladdin Hotel. However, this turned out to be associated with a later recreation from 2022 rather than the original historical advertisement.

After continuing my search, I found a historical photograph shared by Vintage Los Angeles on Facebook. The photograph showed an advertisement for **the Sahara Hotel**, a Las Vegas hotel.

**-** https://www.facebook.com/VintageLosAngeles/photos/before-the-marlboro-man-billboard-see-prior-post-a-giant-add-for-the-sahara-hote/581216968601693/

### Finding
Based on this information we can quite easily say that the name of the hotel that was on the billboard was **SAHARA**.

## 4. Identifying the Replacement of the Marlboro Cowboy
The final objective was to identify the single word prominently displayed on the advertisement that replaced the famous Marlboro cigarette billboard during the 1990s. I searched for information about the removal of the advertisement. This led me to several articles discussing the billboards removal and its replacement with an anti-smoking advertisement.

I used two different sources to make sure I had the right option for it and also because the NYTimes was behind a paywall. I could read part of it in the descrption but not the whole document.

**-** https://www.nytimes.com/1999/05/30/style/party-pooper.html

**-** https://laist.com/news/los-angeles-activities/tobacco-advertising-marlboro-man-sunset-strip

### Finding
The replacement advertisement had one big word on it **IMPOTENT**. This was the final piece we needed for our challenge.

## Final Result
After investigating all of the objectives and cross-referencing sources I was able to answer all of the questions.
| Objective | Finding |
|---|---|
| Historic-Cultural Monument number | 151 |
| Hidden passage destination street number | 8225 |
| Original hotel on the billboard | Sahara |
| Big word on the replacing advertisement | Impotent |

This made the final flag:
